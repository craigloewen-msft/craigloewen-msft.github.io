WatVision aims to empower every blind or visually impaired person to use any touch
screen. It was my Fourth Year Design Project for my Mechatronics Engineering degree at
the University of Waterloo.

The system is made up of an app and a ring that you wear on your finger. You run the
app and point the camera at the touch screen you want to use. The app tells you when it
has seen and understood the screen. From there, you move the finger wearing the ring
around the screen and it reads out whatever is underneath your finger.

### Recognition

The project won the national **James Dyson Award** for all of Canada, the **General
Motors Innovation Award**, and second place in the People's Choice Award.
