SpotMe is a project I started working on in my spare time. The goal was to leverage
machine learning to teach people how to exercise properly with correct form.

In the summer of 2017 I participated in Microsoft's OneWeek hackathon and brought
together a team to build a demo of the project. It was a lot of work — especially as I
was both the lead project manager and the lead developer — but we ended up with a
really strong demo.
